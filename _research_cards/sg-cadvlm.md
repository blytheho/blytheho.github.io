excerpt: "Vision-language models can turn crash reports into simulation scenarios, but they often fall back on generic priors and ignore the report, giving the wrong road layout and tamer collisions. SG-CADVLM uses context-aware decoding to keep the model faithful to the report and its diagram, and generates the road network and vehicle behavior together as files that run in CARLA. On 109 NHTSA crash reports, 88.1% of generated scenarios were critical or high-risk, versus 31.2% for the Omnitester baseline."

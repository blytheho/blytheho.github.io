## Bio

I'm now a Research Assistant at Monash University, advised by [Dr. Zhuo Chen](https://www.monash.edu/engineering/zhuochen#about). My ongoing project focuses on a simulation-based EV charging station optimization.  

Before joining Monash, I got my master's degree at Southeast university and Monash university, advised by [Prof. Ziyuan Pu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EzCLa-4AAAAJ&hl=en). During the 3 years of my master's, I have explored topics like crash data analysis, active transportation and health, safety-critical scenario generation and simulation.
